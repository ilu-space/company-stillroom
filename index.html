Why Stillroom exists

A space designed around
your wellbeing

Helsinki is full of energy — but finding a space where you can truly slow down, think clearly, and recharge has always been harder than it should be. Stillroom was built to change that.

We believe the quality of your daily rituals shapes the quality of your life. When your coffee break becomes a genuine pause — unhurried, beautiful, intentional — everything else gets a little better.

Common questions

Everything you'd want to know

Do I need a membership to visit?

Not at all. Stillroom is open to everyone during regular hours. Membership is for regulars who want the full benefits — unlimited filter drinks, priority seating, and community events.

Can I work here for a full day?

Yes — and that's exactly what the space is designed for. We have quiet work zones, high-speed WiFi, and power outlets. Members get priority access to the bookable meeting nooks.

What kind of food do you serve?

Freshly made pastries, Nordic-inspired open sandwiches, seasonal salads, and plant-based options — all sourced from local Finnish suppliers. Light, nourishing, and never heavy.

What are adaptogen drinks?

Adaptogens are natural herbs and fungi — like ashwagandha, reishi, and lion's mane — that support the body's stress response. We blend them into delicious, functional drinks crafted by our baristas.

How do I book a spot for an event?

Members receive early access and invitations to all events via email. Non-members can check the upcoming events schedule in-store or by signing up for our community newsletter.

Is there dairy-free or plant-based options?

Yes — all drinks can be made with oat, almond, or soy milk, and we always have plant-based food options on the menu. Just let your barista know your preferences.

Can I bring a meeting or small group?

Absolutely. Our bookable meeting nooks are ideal for small professional meetings, creative sessions, or informal team catch-ups. Members can book these in advance.

How do I join the founding member waitlist?

Simply enter your name and email in the membership form above. We'll be in touch before launch with pricing details, founding member perks, and how to get started. No payment required now.
